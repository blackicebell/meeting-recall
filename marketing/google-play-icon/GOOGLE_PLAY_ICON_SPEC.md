# Google Play Requirements

The Google Play listing icon should be:

- 512 x 512 px
- PNG
- 32-bit
- sRGB
- under 1024 KB
- full square
- no transparent background
- no baked rounded corners
- no baked drop shadow

Google Play applies the rounded mask and shadow dynamically.

---

# Design Direction

The icon uses:

- full-bleed dark charcoal background
- centered white radiant recording mark
- red recording dot with a dark red halo
- no wordmark
- no black box
- no generic AI symbols

This keeps the icon readable at small sizes and connects directly to the core product behavior: recording meetings.
