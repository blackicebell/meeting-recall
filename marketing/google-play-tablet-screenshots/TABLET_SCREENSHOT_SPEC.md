# Meeting Recall Google Play Tablet Screenshots

## Purpose

This folder contains Google Play tablet screenshot assets for Meeting Recall.

The goal is to provide tablet-specific store listing screenshots that feel polished, readable, and consistent with the phone screenshot set.

# Google Play Requirements

## 7-inch Tablet

- PNG or JPEG
- up to 8 MB each
- 16:9 or 9:16 aspect ratio
- each side between 320 px and 3840 px

## 10-inch Tablet

- PNG or JPEG
- up to 8 MB each
- 16:9 or 9:16 aspect ratio
- each side between 1080 px and 7680 px

These exports are opaque PNG files and stay within the accepted size/aspect constraints.

# Visual Direction

The tablet screenshots use:

- soft blue/white gradient background
- subtle blue radial shapes
- Meeting Recall brand mark
- large tablet-scale app UI
- direct app surface, not a phone mockup inside a tablet screenshot

Avoid:

- debug UI
- fake product claims
- generic AI visuals
- loud gradients
- crowded text
- phone screenshots inside device frames
