# Meeting Recall Beta Test

Hi, and thank you for helping test Meeting Recall.

Meeting Recall is a simple meeting recorder built to make Google NotebookLM easier to use.

The basic flow is:

Record a meeting -> Save it -> Open NotebookLM -> Upload the recording -> Get summaries and insights

---

# How to Install the APK

1. Download the APK file.
2. Open it on your Android phone.
3. If Android asks for permission to install from this source, allow it.
4. Install Meeting Recall.
5. Open the app and follow the setup screens.

If the install is blocked, your phone may require you to enable installs from unknown apps for the browser, Drive, or Files app you used to open the APK.

---

# What to Test

Please try:

- onboarding
- microphone permission setup
- choosing the Meeting Recall folder
- recording a short meeting or test note
- saving the recording
- finding the recording in Recent Recordings
- playing it back
- opening NotebookLM
- uploading the saved recording
- sharing a recording
- deleting a recording

If you use Google Calendar, also try connecting it and starting from one of today’s meetings.

# How to Send Feedback

Send feedback however is easiest:

- quick text notes
- screenshots
- screen recordings
- voice notes

The most helpful feedback is anything that made you pause, feel unsure, or wonder what to do next.

Please include:

- your phone model
- what you were trying to do
- what happened
- whether you were able to finish the task
